About

CS graduate student at Stevens Institute of Technology, working across embedded systems, applied machine learning, and mobile development. At the WRS Lab I write firmware for wearable sensing hardware on ESP32 and XIAO MG24 in C/C++ — the kind of low-level work that sits a layer below the model.

Before Stevens I was at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham (ECE), where I spent three years in amFOSS — an open-source collective — mentoring contributors and shipping Flutter projects to real users. That overlap of research, product, and community is still how I think about work.

I’ve shipped across every layer: wearable sensing firmware in C/C++, production Flutter apps with 10K+ downloads, ML pipelines for healthcare and wireless security, and an IEEE-published LoRa signal classifier.
