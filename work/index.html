Feb 2026 → present · Hardware / Firmware Research Assistant

Stevens Institute of Technology · WRS Lab

Leading firmware development for wearable sensing systems used in gait monitoring, on ESP32 and XIAO MG24 platforms in C/C++. Work that lives a layer below the model — the data acquisition, signal conditioning, and transmission that makes the ML possible.

Cut SD card write latency from ~2000 ms to ~535 ms on XIAO MG24 by tracing the hardware bottleneck and recommending a platform migration. Reduced WiFi radio current draw by 40–50% on a production ESP32-S3 system while maintaining continuous real-time UDP comms and SD logging. Serving as the lab’s point of contact for the Simbex partnership.
